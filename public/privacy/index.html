Privacy

tayseerlaz.com, the portfolio of Tayseer Laz, product developer in Beirut, Lebanon. Last updated 26 October 2026.

In short

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising. Fonts and images are served from this site; the only outside file it loads on its own is the OmniSign case study’s video, hosted on Cloudflare R2.

What stays in your browser

The sticky notes on the home page remember your edits and pins in your browser’s local storage. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Contact forms

The postcard and the old-Mac contact window do not send anything to a server. They open a new message in your own email app, and nothing is sent until you press send there.

Server logs

Like any web server, the host keeps standard access logs (IP address, browser and pages requested) for security and operations. They are not used to profile visitors.

Links to other sites

Share buttons, profile links and project links open other websites (such as LinkedIn, X, Facebook, WhatsApp or GitHub) only when you click them. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
